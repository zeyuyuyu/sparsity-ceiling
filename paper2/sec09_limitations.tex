\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Simulation, not silicon.}
Every energy figure in this paper is the 45\,nm Horowitz MAC/AC proxy applied to counted
operations, not a measurement of any device. The analog state is dense (state density
$0.994$), and each hidden unit implies an analog storage element and a converter that
MAC/AC counting alone does not price; Section~\ref{sec:hardware-stranded} prices both
under the proxy and finds them at $0.00$--$0.33\%$ of the analog total at $H{=}256$, but
that assessment inherits every assumption of the proxy itself. Matching variants on
\emph{emitted} rate matches wire traffic, not
state-holding cost (Section~\ref{sec:metrics}). The $1.60\times$ figure of
Section~\ref{sec:charlm-claim} is a claim about communication and arithmetic under the
proxy, and could shrink or invert once storage and conversion are priced on real silicon;
measured Loihi-2/SpiNNaker-2-class energy is the direct next step.

\paragraph{Small scale, short schedules.}
The char-LM models have $173{,}596$ parameters trained for six epochs; the copy models
span $10.5$k--$87.9$k parameters at thirty epochs. Effects that are
regularization-mediated at this scale may attenuate at larger scale or longer schedules.
This cuts in both directions: the training-time noise benefit on the digital baseline
($-0.309$ bpc, Section~\ref{sec:charlm-control}) is exactly the kind of small-model,
short-schedule effect that typically shrinks as models grow --- but the analog route's
$+0.160$ bpc gating cost is measured against that same baseline, so the surviving
tradeoff could move in either direction at scale. Absolute task quality is far from
state of the art by construction (a one-layer diagonal SSM), which is the price of the
parameter-matched protocol.

\paragraph{Two tasks, one out-of-sample test.}
The ranking inversion rests on one statistical task (char-level WikiText) and one
precise-recall task (synthetic copy). The datapath-degradation principle was tested
out of sample exactly once --- the quantizer contrast of
Section~\ref{sec:principle-quantizer}, $-0.002$ bpc on char-LM versus $+0.369$/$+0.385$
bpc on copy at $M{=}16$/$32$ --- and that test is post hoc: the pre-registered arm
turned out to be a training-time-only regularizer and could not test the principle as
designed. The genuinely pre-registered element is the $M{=}32$ floor
($\geq{+}0.37$ bpc, met). A designed, pre-registered test on a third workload family
(e.g.\ associative recall with distractors, or a streaming perception task) is the
obvious strengthening experiment.

\paragraph{The regularized reference is not an exhaustively tuned one.}
All corrected copy margins are quoted against a digital baseline given training-time
state noise at $\sigma{=}0.02$ --- the dose at which the char-LM benefit saturated ---
not against a baseline tuned over a broad regularizer grid. Weight decay was worth
exactly zero ($+0.005\pm0.011$ bpc), which narrows the mechanism but does not exhaust
the space; a stronger baseline would shrink every neuromorphic margin further, and no
result in this paper moves in the neuromorphic routes' favour if the baseline improves.

\paragraph{Scope of the bound-arm negative.}
Section~\ref{sec:bound} shows the firing-floor bound's width prediction fails on
\emph{this} architecture at \emph{this} scale --- a one-layer LIF-state SSM whose copy
failure is an optimization pathology. That is a tested scope limit, not a general
refutation: an architecture that actually trains to retain $M\log_2 K$ bits in a spiking
state could still exhibit the floor. We could not construct one, and report that fact
rather than the bound's failure in general.

\paragraph{Validity limit at the heaviest load.}
The copy $M{=}64$ column is capacity-limited for every variant (the digital reference
itself is only $2.7\times$ chance) and has no regularized reference, so it supports only
the chance-level reproduction of the spiking-state result; the load-scaling claims of
Section~\ref{sec:copy-load} are scoped to $M{=}16\!\to\!32$.

\paragraph{Scope of the stranded-readout negative.}
The readout-energy results of Section~\ref{sec:hardware-stranded} carry three designed
limits. The attribution of the copy collapse to staleness is an inference from the
char-LM ablation --- the random-hold arm was not run on copy. The cited operating points
are three-seed, but the intermediate thresholds and hold rates between them remain
single-seed, and the low-rate delta-versus-random comparison exists on the digital arm
only. And the $1.74\times$ free-recurrence ceiling is a property of this model's shape:
the readout dominates because $H \times V$ exceeds $H^{2}$ at this vocabulary, so the
decomposition must be redone before applying the conclusion to a differently proportioned
SSM (at $H \gg V$ the recurrence, not the readout, would carry the energy).
